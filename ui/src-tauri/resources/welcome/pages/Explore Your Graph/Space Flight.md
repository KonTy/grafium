# Explore Your Graph / Space Flight

## Your first flight

1. Open **Graph** and switch to **3D**.
2. Search for **Space** to choose a starting topic.
3. Select **Space flight** and let the camera follow links and orbit destinations.
4. Select **Stop flight**, or press **Esc**, to return to manual control and the normal graph layout.

Changing graphs or leaving the view also stops the flight.

## What you are seeing

Pages become named, solid-colored planets. Some main planets have rings. Visible slash-namespace children become smaller, ringless satellites around their nearest visible ancestor.

This graph has a real three-level example: [[Space]], [[Space/Orbits]], and [[Space/Orbits/Gravity]]. The observatory's plan and log offer another system beneath [[Projects/Observatory Night]].

Namespace shades help related planets look like a family during flight. The star field and locally bundled NASA galaxy images continue behind the scene.

**This is a view, not a rewrite.** Satellite positions and display-only hierarchy flight routes do not create or alter stored links. Stopping restores the normal layout described in [[Explore Your Graph]].
