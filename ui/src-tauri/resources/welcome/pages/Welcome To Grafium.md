**Start with one idea. Give it somewhere to grow.**

A note can become a plan, a question can become a flashcard, and a few links can become a map. This Welcome Graph is an editable playground, separate from any graph you create.

## Before you begin

- **Privacy first:** Grafium stores your Markdown and graph data locally. AI is
  optional. A cloud AI provider receives the prompt and note context you choose
  to send, so start with [[AI Setup And Privacy]].
- **Sync second:** Sync keeps separate copies on USB drives, file servers, or
  WebDAV. Configure each target independently and read [[Sync And Privacy]]
  before connecting a real graph.

## Try three things

1. **Make a mark.** Open [[Start With One Idea]] and change the first practice block.
2. **See a connection.** Follow its link to [[Space/Orbits]], then look at that page's linked references.
3. **Take flight.** Open **Graph**, switch to **3D**, search for Space, and try **Space flight**.

## Choose your next direction

- **Write and organize** — [[Writing]]: nested notes, rich formatting, sortable tables, and finding things again.
- **Turn notes into action** — [[Projects]]: a small observatory-night plan, tasks, and a live dashboard.
- **Learn something** — [[Learning]]: reading notes, a collection, and ready-to-review flashcards.
- **Explore connections** — [[Explore Your Graph]]: 2D communities, a free 3D camera, and guided flights.
- **Make room for your day** — [[Journal And Calendar]]: today, yesterday, or a date you choose.
- **Make it yours** — [[Your Workspace]]: appearance, files, imports, optional AI, and your own graph.
- **Use AI carefully** — [[AI Setup And Privacy]]: providers, models, embeddings, local versus cloud processing, and privacy.
- **Sync safely** — [[Sync And Privacy]]: USB, file servers, WebDAV, multiple targets, conflicts, and backups.

**Nothing here needs an account or a download to explore.** Optional AI and media-processing features have their own setup. Keep experimenting here, or use [[Create Your Own Graph]] when you are ready.
