# Learning / Flashcards

A card is an ordinary block written as `Front :: Back` on one physical line. The examples below are real cards; their #Space topic connects them to the subject, not to a universal demo category.

- What bends the path of an object in a simple gravitational orbit? :: Gravity continually changes its direction of motion. #Space
- What does a telescope's aperture measure? :: The diameter of its main light-collecting opening. #Space
- Is a light-year a unit of time or distance? :: Distance: how far light travels in one year. #Space
- Which two ingredients are shown in this orbit sketch? :: Inward gravity and sideways motion. ![Original orbit sketch](../assets/welcome/orbit.svg) #Space

## Review, then return to the idea

Open **Flashcards** from the sidebar. Study the Space topic or choose **Mixed**. Reveal an answer, grade your recall with **Again**, **Hard**, **Good**, or **Easy**, and Grafium schedules the next review using spaced repetition.

Keep questions specific enough to judge your answer. A card can contain rich Markdown and a real local image, as this page does. The explanation behind these cards lives in [[Space/Orbits]], [[Space/Telescopes]], and [[Space/Light]].

## Bring your existing cards

Use **Import Anki deck** in Flashcards to choose an `.apkg` file. Imported cards become editable notes, with supported deck media copied into the graph.

Answers can also include local audio or video when you supply the files. No sample audio, video, or external media download is required by this graph. See [[Imports And Media]] for the optional tools.
