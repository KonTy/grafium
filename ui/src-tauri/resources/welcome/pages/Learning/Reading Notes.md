# Learning / Reading Notes

## Question

Why does an orbit need both gravity and sideways motion?

## Explain it in your own words

- Gravity continually changes the direction of motion.
- Sideways motion carries the object onward while it falls.
- In an ideal circular orbit, the path keeps curving around the central body.

That is a model, not the full story of every real orbit. Track assumptions and unanswered questions alongside your explanation.
