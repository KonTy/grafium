## Links worth keeping

Grafium can surface link candidates for review. Inspect a candidate before choosing **Link**, or review a group before using **Link all**. When **Fix + link** is offered, check the proposed repair as well as the connection. Choose **Not an edge** for an unrelated candidate, or use **Undo** after accepting a connection you did not want.

Suggestions are not established facts. Accepting a connection is different from merely displaying a suggestion: review what it connects before adding it to your notes.
