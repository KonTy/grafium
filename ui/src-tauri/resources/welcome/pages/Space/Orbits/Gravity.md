In a simple two-body model, gravitational acceleration points toward the other body. For a circular orbit it changes the direction of velocity without changing its speed.

$$
a = \frac{GM}{r^2}
$$

Here $r$ is measured from the central body's center, not its surface. The model assumes a dominant central mass and leaves out effects such as drag.

Ask yourself: if gravity vanished, would the object keep following the same circle? In this idealized model it would move along the tangent instead.
