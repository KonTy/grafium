# Explore Your Graph

Open **Graph** to see notes as a network. Switch between **2D** and **3D**: both use the same pages and accepted connections. Date pages and their links are included by default.

## Read the neighborhoods

These examples contain several connected groups: writing, a small project, learning, and space. Densely linked pages tend to gather; weaker bridges stretch farther between groups.

The layout uses weighted, Louvain-style link communities—not semantic AI categories. Colors describe the loaded graph's connections. Changing the node limit or local/global scope can change the communities you see. Suggested connections are separate from accepted links and do not define the groups.

## Explore by hand

- In **2D**, pan and zoom, drag a node, and hover to reveal titles and connections. Search for a page to find it in the map.
- In **3D**, rotate and move the free camera, zoom, and inspect solid sphere nodes. Unconnected nodes sit on a spherical surface rather than being assigned an invented topic.
- Compare the local neighborhood of a page with the global view. Increase the node limit if an expected page is outside the loaded set.

The stars and distant NASA galaxy images are decoration, not notes. The images are bundled locally and remain visible in normal 3D and during flight; opening this graph does not fetch a sky background.
