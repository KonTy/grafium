# Help - Printing

Press **Ctrl+P** / **Cmd+P**, or choose **Print** from the app menu, to print
the page you are reading or save it as a PDF. Printing works on an ordinary
page and on a journal day; open one first, because the other screens have
nothing page-shaped to print.

## What gets printed

Grafium prints from your saved notes, not from the screen. Folded sections,
blocks scrolled out of view, and the parts of a long page that have not been
drawn yet are all included, so a printout is never shorter than the page.

The print window offers whichever of these apply:

- **This whole page** - everything on the page, in order.
- **One chapter** - a heading and everything belonging to it, up to the next
  heading of the same or a higher level. This works both for imported books,
  where chapters are a run of `#` headings on one long page, and for notes
  where you have indented the content under its heading.
- **The selected blocks** - offered when you have blocks selected before you
  press Print. Anything nested inside a selected block comes with it.

Unsaved edits are saved first, so you print the words you can see.

## Paper is always white

Whatever theme you use, printed pages come out white with dark text. A dark or
OLED theme never floods the sheet with ink or prints pale grey text that is
almost invisible on paper.

Choose the ink you want:

- **Colour** keeps links, callouts and pictures in colour.
- **Black and white** prints text, rules and backgrounds in plain black on
  white, and converts pictures to grey. Pictures are kept rather than dropped,
  so they still show their detail.

The preview in the print window shows the real printed page, including this
colour choice, before anything is sent to the printer.

## Save as PDF

**Save as PDF** writes the same document straight to a file you choose. It is
the same rendering the printer would produce, so the preview applies to it too.

You can also reach a PDF through **Print** by picking your system's
*Print to File* destination, if you prefer the system print window.

## How the printed page is laid out

- App chrome - sidebars, toolbars, buttons and the navigation bar - is left
  out. Only the document is printed.
- Headings stay with the text that follows them instead of being stranded at
  the foot of a page, and a block is kept on one page where it fits.
- Long code lines wrap instead of running off the edge of the sheet, and wide
  pictures and tables are scaled to fit.
- Nested notes keep their structure, shown with bullets and indentation. A flat
  page, such as an imported book, prints as plain prose with no bullets.
- Audio, video and embedded players cannot be printed, so they appear as a
  short note naming the file rather than as a blank gap.

## Shortcut

| Action | Shortcut |
| --- | --- |
| Print | `Ctrl+P` / `Cmd+P` |

You can change this in **Settings - Shortcuts**, and **Print** is also in the
command palette.
