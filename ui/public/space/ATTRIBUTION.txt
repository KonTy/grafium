GRAFIUM UNIVERSE BACKGROUND IMAGES
================================
These are local derivatives of real Hubble Space Telescope observations, not
generated galaxy illustrations. They decorate the sky only; their positions,
orientations, scale, colors and brightness are not an astronomical sky map.
No endorsement by NASA, ESA, STScI or AURA is claimed or implied.

LOCAL PROCESSING
  Downloaded from the official URLs above on 2026-09-13.
  Reduced to 512 pixels wide with Pillow's Lanczos resampling; sizes are 512x356
  (Whirlpool) and 512x284 (Sombrero). Low-level black sky was removed, luminosity
  moved into alpha, and elliptical plus edge feathering applied. All outermost
  pixels are fully transparent. RGB is unpremultiplied for soft alpha compositing;
  the shader further reduces brightness to preserve foreground graph readability.
  Encoded as WebP, quality 90, method 6, with transparency. Total image size is
  201,954 bytes. Presentation is intentionally modified from the scientific images.
